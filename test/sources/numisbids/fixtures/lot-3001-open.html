Betts Medals
1580 Medal Spanish Conquest of Portugal, Silver, Betts-11 -- Tooled -- PCGS Genuine. AU Details. Light-silver surfaces contain hints of russet. When two successive kings of Portugal died without heirs, a continent-wide battle for control of Portugal began. King Philip II of Spain took Portugal by force, becoming Philip I of Portugal upon the fall of Lisbon. This had large implications for the New World, as Portuguese and Spanish claims, divided since the times of Columbus were unified under the Spanish crown. Many experts agree that this medal is a well-made 18th century copy rather than a contemporary issue. From The Costa Family Collection, Part III. www.HA.com/TexasAuctioneerLicenseNotice
